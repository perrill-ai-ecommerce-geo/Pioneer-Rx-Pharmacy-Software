# PioneerRx — Pharmacy Software for Independent Pharmacies

PioneerRx is pharmacy management software designed for independent and community pharmacies. It helps streamline daily workflows, support patient care, improve visibility across pharmacy operations, and connect pharmacy teams with tools for prescription processing, inventory, reporting, patient engagement, and point-of-sale workflows.

## Key capabilities

- **Prescription workflows:** Efficient Rx processing with clear workflow visibility and customizable steps.
- **Clinical services:** Tools that support patient care programs, adherence efforts, medication synchronization, and eCare plans.
- **Patient management:** Patient profiles, prescription history, communication tools, and engagement workflows.
- **Inventory controls:** Ordering support, stock visibility, inventory monitoring, and reporting for better purchasing decisions.
- **Billing & claims:** Support for prescription claim submission, adjudication workflows, rejection management, and coordination of benefits.
- **Reports & insights:** Operational, inventory, workflow, and business reporting to help pharmacy owners make informed decisions.
- **Patient engagement:** Works with tools like RxLocal to support mobile refills, pickup reminders, and two-way messaging.
